# ApplyPilot

An open-source (AGPL-3.0) Python tool that finds jobs, scores them against your
resume, tailors your resume and cover letter per job, and **submits applications
autonomously** through headless [[claude-code|Claude Code]] browser sessions
([[2026-09-27-applypilot]]).

## Key facts
- v0.3.0; last commit 2026-03-08; about 9.6K lines of Python
  ([[2026-09-27-applypilot]]).
- Scrapes major job boards and employer career sites; optional paid CAPTCHA
  solving ([[2026-09-27-applypilot]]).
- The name is also used by unrelated commercial sites ([[2026-09-27-applypilot]]).

## Your evaluation
See [[applypilot-monetization]]. Short version: not worth selling as is. A
human-in-the-loop career copilot for clinicians is the more viable idea
*(inference)*.
