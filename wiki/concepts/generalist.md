# Generalist

Someone whose value comes from **breadth**: working across several domains and
connecting them, rather than going deep in one. The vault owner describes themselves
this way ([[2026-09-26-vault-focus]]).

## Why it matters for this vault
A generalist's knowledge is spread over many fields, so the hard part is keeping it
organised and finding links between fields. An [[llm-wiki]] suits this because
cross-referencing is automatic *(inference)*.
