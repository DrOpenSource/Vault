# Maintenance Burden

The observation that personal and team wikis are abandoned because **the cost of
upkeep grows faster than the value they deliver**. The tedious part is not reading or
thinking but bookkeeping: updating cross-references, keeping summaries current,
noting contradictions, maintaining consistency across dozens of pages
([[2026-09-26-llm-wiki-pattern]]).

## Why LLMs change the equation
LLMs "don't get bored, don't forget to update a cross-reference, and can touch 15
files in one pass," so maintenance cost approaches zero and the wiki stays alive
([[2026-09-26-llm-wiki-pattern]]).
