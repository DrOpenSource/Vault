# Personal Productivity

Getting more of the work that matters done, with less friction. It is one of the two
stated goals of this vault ([[2026-09-26-vault-focus]]).

## Role of the vault
The vault supports productivity by doing the note-keeping upkeep
([[maintenance-burden]]) and by making past learning easy to find again, so you don't
redo the same work *(inference)*.
