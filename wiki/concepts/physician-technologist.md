# Physician-Technologist

A professional profile that combines **clinical expertise with hands-on product and
technology execution**. One person can move from a clinician's real problem to a
spec, an AI build, a clinical and technical check of the output, and a release, with
"no translation layer needed between product and engineering" ([[2026-09-26-resume]]).
This is how [[me|you]] position yourself.

## The value chain
| Step | Clinical skill it draws on | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Understand the real problem | Time on the front line (ED, ICU) | [[2026-09-26-resume]] |
| Frame it | Turning pain points into problem statements and evaluation criteria | [[athira-health]] |
| Specify it | Turning clinical guidelines into product logic | [[grow-baby-grow]], [[saathi]] |
| Build it | AI coding agents, automation | [[ai-assisted-development]], [[n8n]] |
| Judge the output | Clinical soundness and safety | [[2026-09-26-resume]] |
| Ship within constraints | Regulatory and medico-legal fluency | [[docbot]], [[stemz-healthcare]] |

## Why it may matter
In healthcare AI, the costly failures are often clinically plausible but wrong
outputs, and workflows that don't fit how care is delivered. A builder who can judge
both may cut iteration cycles and risk *(inference)*.
