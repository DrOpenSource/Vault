# Memex

A hypothetical personal knowledge device described by [[vannevar-bush|Vannevar Bush]]
in 1945: a personal, curated store of documents linked by **associative trails**
([[2026-09-26-llm-wiki-pattern]]). Bush introduced it in the essay "As We May Think"
*(background)*.

## Relation to this vault
The idea file argues the Memex is closer in spirit to an [[llm-wiki]] than to what the
web became: private, actively curated, with the connections between documents as
valuable as the documents themselves. What Bush couldn't solve was who maintains it —
the LLM fills that role ([[2026-09-26-llm-wiki-pattern]]).
