## TL;DR
A physician (MBBS) with **10+ years in clinical and healthcare leadership** in
emergency medicine, critical care and multi-site operations, who since 2023 has
**shipped AI products end to end independently**: 12 Android apps, 60+ n8n automation
pipelines, and health apps built against WHO, AIIMS, IAP and ICMR-NIN guidelines. He
positions himself as an **AI Product Manager / Clinical Strategy /
Physician-Technologist**: someone who can take a clinician's problem, write the spec,
judge whether AI output is sound, and ship it with "no translation layer needed".
