# Overview

*The evolving big-picture synthesis of everything in this vault. Rewritten (not
appended) as sources accumulate.*

## What this vault is
A second brain built on the [[llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]] pattern: you curate raw sources and
ask questions; the LLM compiles them into interlinked pages and keeps them current
([[2026-09-26-llm-wiki-pattern]]). Browse it in [[obsidian|Obsidian]].

## Current themes

## Evolving thesis
Maintenance, not insight, is what kills knowledge bases; making maintenance nearly
free is what makes a personal wiki sustainable ([[2026-09-26-llm-wiki-pattern]]).
